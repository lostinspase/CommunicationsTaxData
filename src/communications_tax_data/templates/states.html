What “pulled” means

A healthy site check proves only that an official page is reachable. A rule is “pulled” only after a state-specific parser has produced a normalized fact with an effective date, tax base or taxability treatment, and legal/source citation. “Partial” means work has started; it does not mean the state is calculation-ready.
